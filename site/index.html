Chess Lab Open

A free, open-source chess trainer: openings against real games, tactics, calculation, and drills from your own Lichess or Chess.com games. It all runs in your browser; nothing is uploaded. By Suhaas Narayanan (CoachSuhaas on Chess.com, suhaasn on Lichess).

New here?

How Chess Lab Open works

  • Start with your own games. In Analyze my games, type your Lichess or Chess.com username: you get a report card and drills from your mistakes (and the ones your opponents got away with).
  • Come back to Today. A short daily session: a few of your drills, five puzzles near your rating, one endgame, one opening line.
  • Openings: pick an opening or paste your repertoire as a PGN. The opponent plays real moves from Lichess games at the level you set, from 1000 to Elite, then Stockfish takes over.
  • Players: type an opponent’s username to see what they play, train against their openings and print a pre-round sheet. Watch: your own video library, with your place, notes and bookmarks.
  • Tactics, Calculation, Endgames, Play and Guess the move (add your own games too) for everything else.
  • On every board: right-click or right-drag for circles and arrows (Shift red, Alt or Ctrl blue). Settings → Theme switches to dark.
  • Private by design: no account, nothing uploaded. Your progress stays in this browser (Progress → Export to move it).

The idea

Analyze my games

Fetches your recent rapid and classical games straight from the Lichess or Chess.com public API — in your browser; nothing is uploaded anywhere. Lichess games reuse Lichess’s own server analysis where it exists; Chess.com has no stored evals, so the last 25 games are analyzed with Stockfish in your browser instead (slower).

Made by Analyze my games from your own games: Your mistakes (find the move you should have played) and Punish the mistake (your opponent erred; find the move that makes them pay).

Settings

Engine: Stockfish 18 (lite, WebAssembly), GPL-3.0 — served with this site; source at github.com/official-stockfish/Stockfish and nmrugg/stockfish.js. Book data: the Lichess Elite Database and the Lichess open database (CC0), built into static books that ship with the site. See CREDITS.
