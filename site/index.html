Chess Lab Open

An open-source chess trainer that runs on your own games: train your openings against the masters’ book, sharpen tactics, and get a report card from your Lichess or Chess.com games — all in your browser, nothing uploaded. By Suhaas Narayanan (CoachSuhaas on Chess.com, suhaasn on Lichess).

About this puzzle

Analyze my games

Fetches your recent rapid and classical games straight from the Lichess or Chess.com public API — in your browser; nothing is uploaded anywhere. Lichess games reuse Lichess’s own server analysis where it exists; Chess.com has no stored evals, so the last 25 games are analyzed with Stockfish in your browser instead (slower).

Drills are made from your own mistakes by Analyze my games: find the move you should have played.
